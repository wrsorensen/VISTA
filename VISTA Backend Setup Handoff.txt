Setup Steps
1. Open the Google Sheet.

5. Save.
6. Run setupVistaSheets_ once from Apps Script.
7. Approve the Google authorization prompts.
8. Go to Deploy > New deployment.
9. Choose Web app.
10. Use these deployment settings:
    Execute as: Me
    Who has access: Anyone
11. Deploy.
12. Copy the Web app URL.
13. Send that URL back to the VISTA build chat.
